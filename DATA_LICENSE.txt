The adapted map database is distributed under ODbL 1.0 and is available in machine-readable form at ./data/district.json on this same site. Geometry is projected in EPSG:32632 relative to the origin recorded in that file. Retained OSM identifiers and metadata describe the underlying mapped facts and approximations.

Buildings and terrain are simplified. Heights, bridge clearances, ramps and landmark forms may be illustrative. This is a prototype, not a surveyed model and not for navigation.
